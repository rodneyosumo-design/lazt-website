Got questions?

What admissions teams ask first.

How is this different from a 360° virtual tour?

A 360° tour is a series of fixed photos. LAZT is one continuous, photoreal 3D space that students move through freely, as your mascot, with your story built in.

Does it replace our in-person visit?

No. It's built to get more students to one. Every tour points to Schedule a Visit, and quests can reward students who come see campus in person.

What data do you collect?

Anonymous usage analytics only: which stops hold attention, where students drop off, and how many tap Request Info, Visit, or Apply. The tour doesn't collect names, emails, or other personal data.

How much of campus do you capture?

Pilots usually start with four signature zones, such as your main quad, chapel, academic core, and athletics, and grow from there.

Can we use our mascot?

Yes. We build your mascot as the playable character under a license from your school, and your team approves the look.

Where do you work?

We're based in Fairfield, Connecticut, and travel to campuses for capture.
